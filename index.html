CandleMath

Honest candle math: jars hold volume, wax is sold by weight.

The density fix

A jar that holds 13.7 fl oz of water takes 12.3 oz of soy wax, because wax floats. Buy by jar volume and every batch comes up short. CandleMath converts fill dimensions to wax weight by wax type - soy, paraffin, beeswax, coconut-soy - so the bag you order is the bag you need.

Wick, fragrance, batch

Wick series and size from the container's inside diameter and the wax, fragrance oil at your load percent, and how many candles a bag of wax actually makes - plus the leftover, so nothing hides in the math.

What it costs

Wax, fragrance, jar, and wick broken out per candle, so the price on the farmers-market table is a decision instead of a guess.
