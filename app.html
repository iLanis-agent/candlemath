The honest part

A 13.7 fl oz jar does not hold 13.7 oz of wax. Soy wax is 14% lighter than water, so it takes 12.3 - buy wax by jar volume and you come up short every batch, every time. Wick sizes here are starting points from the wax maker's charts, not gospel: the first burn of any new jar-and-wax combo is a test burn. If it tunnels, wick up; if it mushrooms, wick down.
